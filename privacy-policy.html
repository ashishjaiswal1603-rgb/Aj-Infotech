Privacy Policy

AJ Infotech · Effective date: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AJ Infotech handles information when you contact us or request digital application, documentation, registration, booking or related assistance.

1. Information we may receive

  • Name, mobile number and email address.
  • Address and identity details needed for the service you request.
  • Documents and application information you choose to provide.
  • Payment reference or transaction details needed to confirm a payment.

2. How information is used

We use information to understand your request, prepare or submit the service you ask for where authorised, communicate with you, share application updates, deliver completed documents and resolve payment or service queries.

3. Document handling and retention

We aim to use customer documents only for the requested purpose and restrict access to people who need them to handle that request. Working copies are intended to be deleted after the service is completed, except where retention is required by applicable law, a government process, accounting requirements, or to resolve a genuine dispute. Please do not send documents that are not needed for your requested service.

4. Sharing with service providers

Information may be submitted to the relevant government department, official portal, authorised service provider, insurer, travel provider or payment provider when necessary to carry out the service you requested. Those organisations may handle information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell customer personal information.

5. Keeping information secure

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Customers should send documents only through contact channels they have verified as belonging to AJ Infotech.

Never share your UPI PIN, ATM PIN, banking password or email password. Do not share an OTP unless you understand the official action it authorises and it is genuinely required on an official process. AJ Infotech will never ask for your UPI PIN or banking password.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask what information you have provided, request correction of inaccurate contact details, or ask about deletion of documents, subject to legal and service-related retention requirements.

7. Third-party websites and services

Links, government portals, WhatsApp, payment services and other third-party platforms are governed by their respective terms and privacy practices. AJ Infotech does not control their systems.
